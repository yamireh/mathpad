# v1.2.8

_Date: 2026-07-25_

First-run welcome tips — help kids discover the how-to lightbulb.

## Fixes & changes

### Onboarding / tips
- **New welcome tips card** on the practice home screen. Three quick tips with
  icons:
  - 💡 tap the lightbulb on any topic to watch how that mode works (kids often
    didn't notice it);
  - ✎ a pen/stylus is easier and neater than a finger;
  - ▦ write one digit per box — MathPen reads the handwriting.
- **Shows on every launch until the kid taps "Don't show again"** (persisted);
  "Got it!" / back just closes it for the session.
- **Reopenable anytime** from **Grown-ups → Tips & how-to**.
- Two clear buttons: **Got it!** (primary) and **Don't show this again**
  (secondary) — replaces an easy-to-miss dim text link.
