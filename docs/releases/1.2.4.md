# v1.2.4

_Date: 2026-07-15_

Home-screen mascot, pinned/tightened headers, and restore-purchase polish.

> Backfilled from commit history (this release predates the per-version
> release-notes convention).

## Fixes & changes

### Mascot & home
- **Home hero now shows the app mascot** (transparent-cut art) instead of the
  app icon.
- Added the **`MascotReaction` UI primitive** (animated, reduce-motion aware) —
  built for future use; only the static hero art is used for now.

### Header spacing
- **Pinned the Operations header** (it used to scroll away with the cards).
- Tightened the gap under the pinned header on **Home, Clock settings, and
  Operations** (`xl` → `md` top padding).

### Purchases
- **Restore now works across devices / Family Sharing in place** — it polls for
  StoreKit's async-delivered transactions, so a restored purchase unlocks
  without an app restart.
- Reworded the Restore button + added a restore hint on both unlock screens.

## Store "What's New" (as shipped)

> • Say hello to the MathPen mascot on the home screen.
> • Fixed restoring purchases across your devices and Family Sharing.
> • Layout polish across the app.
